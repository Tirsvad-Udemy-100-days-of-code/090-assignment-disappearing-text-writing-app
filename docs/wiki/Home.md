# Disappearing Text Writing App

A small Flask web app for writers who get stuck: type into the page, and if you stop for more than 5 seconds, everything you wrote disappears. It is the Day 90 assignment of Udemy's *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*.
